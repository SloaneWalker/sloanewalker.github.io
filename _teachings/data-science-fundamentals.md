## Course Overview

Students learn the following skills: 

- Learn basic data types, interpretations, and uses. 
- Gain experience in creating various plots to visualize data.
- Gain introductory experience in R.

## Prerequisites

- One of any grade 12 (or 40S) Mathematics (50%), MATH 1018, or MSKL 0100
